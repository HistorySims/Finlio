# Finlay, Erin & Colin

A side-scrolling platformer where Finlay (5), Erin (3) and baby Colin run through their craftsman house and out the front door.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step or install.

## How to play

- Pick who leads. The other two follow along behind; only the leader touches anything.
- **← →** walk, **Space** jump, **X** run (and blow bubbles once you have the bubble wand), **C** swap leader, **P** pause, **M** sound.
- On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen buttons.

Hop on turtles, goldfish and frogs. Kick turtle shells. Watch out for the fire-puffing dandelions in the terracotta pots, and remember: the floor is lava.

Toy boxes hold Cheerios, a super cape, a bubble wand and a rainbow popsicle. One of the alphabet blocks in the kitchen hides a teddy bear (extra life).
